01 / ABOUT

Curiosity for parasites.
Commitment to health.

I am a Doctor of Veterinary Medicine graduate from Bangladesh Agricultural University and currently pursuing a Master’s degree in Preventive Veterinary Medicine at Chungbuk National University, South Korea, as a Global Korea Scholarship scholar.

My academic interests centre on veterinary parasitology and infectious diseases, with particular interest in molecular identification, epidemiology, host–parasite relationships and the zoonotic implications of pathogens. I am motivated by research that connects laboratory investigation with practical animal and public-health needs.

I am developing my research experience in parasite molecular biology, including work involving goat coccidia and molecular characterization of Eimeria. I am also interested in immunology, macrophage biology and immune responses to infection.
